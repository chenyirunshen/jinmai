About Jinmai

A company that turns "fake flowers" into real art

Suqian Jinmai Craft Products Co., Ltd. is based in Suqian, Jiangsu — an artificial-flower manufacturer integrating R&D, production and sales. Guided by the idea of "restoring nature, serving life," we combine traditional handcraft with modern manufacturing to bring artificial flowers into homes and public spaces alike.

  • Own production line with strict quality control from raw material to finished product
  • In-house R&D team continuously launches trend-led designs
  • Online & offline integrated procurement and wholesale customization
  • Complete after-sales and logistics for peace of mind
