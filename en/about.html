About Suqian Jinmai

Starting from a single flower, we turn our love for nature into craftsmanship that stays with life.

Company Profile

Focused on artificial flowers, a friend of time

Suqian Jinmai Craft Products Co., Ltd. was founded in Suqian, Jiangsu — a craft hub — as an artificial-flower enterprise integrating R&D, production and sales. We believe beauty should not wither with time: artificial flowers keep blossoms eternal and greenery ever present.

Over more than a decade, Jinmai has grown from a small workshop into a manufacturer with its own production line and a mature R&D team. Our products reach over thirty provinces and cities across China and are exported to multiple regions overseas, serving homes, weddings, hotels, malls and municipal landscapes.

  • Own factory for source-direct supply with controlled quality and lead time
  • Hundreds of new designs launched each year, tracking global floral trends
  • OEM / ODM and sample-based customization supported
